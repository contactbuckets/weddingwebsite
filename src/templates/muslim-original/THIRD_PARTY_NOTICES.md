# Original Muslim wedding invitation template

Source: https://github.com/karthikrshet/wedding-invitation

The original author granted ShaadiSutra commercial reuse permission by direct text message, including the original photos, video and music assets. The user confirmed this permission in the project conversation on 19 November 2026.

Original React/TypeScript components, visual design and media references are retained. Host-runtime compatibility changes are limited to a Vite/React 18 image adapter, asset URL paths, Tailwind v3 stylesheet entry, and dynamic wedding-data adapter.
